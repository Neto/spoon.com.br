How to install

  1. Open the file you downloaded. Your Mac unzips it into WhatsTime.app.
  2. Drag WhatsTime.app into your Applications folder.
  3. Open WhatsTime from your Applications folder. Its icon shows in the Dock and in the menu bar.

Set it up once

  1. Install WhatsApp for Mac from the App Store or whatsapp.com, and log in.
  2. In WhatsTime, click Open Privacy & Security. In Accessibility, turn on WhatsTime. This lets WhatsTime press Send in WhatsApp.
  3. When WhatsTime asks for Contacts, click Allow if you want to pick people by name. You can also type any number.
  4. Click New Message, choose the person, write the message, add files and set the time. Then click Schedule.

Keep your Mac on and WhatsApp logged in. The display can be off. If a message cannot go out, it flashes in WhatsTime and you can send it with one click.
