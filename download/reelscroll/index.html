How to install

  1. Open the file you downloaded. Your Mac unzips it into ReelScroll.app.
  2. Drag ReelScroll.app into your Applications folder.
  3. Open ReelScroll from your Applications folder. Its icon shows in the Dock and in the menu bar.

Set up your browser once

  1. In the ReelScroll menu, choose your browser: Chrome, Brave, Edge or Safari.
  2. In Chrome, Brave or Edge, choose View > Developer > Allow JavaScript from Apple Events. In Safari, open Settings > Advanced and turn on Show features for web developers. Then choose Develop > Developer Settings > Allow JavaScript from Apple Events.
  3. Turn on Auto scroll in the ReelScroll menu. When macOS asks, allow ReelScroll to control your browser.
  4. Click Open Insta Reels, or open Instagram Reels yourself. The next Reel starts when a video ends.

If you clicked Don't Allow by mistake, go to System Settings, Privacy & Security, Automation, and turn ReelScroll on for your browser.
