How to install

  1. Open the file you downloaded. Your Mac unzips it into Cena.app.
  2. Drag Cena.app into your Applications folder.
  3. Open Cena from your Applications folder.

Set it up once

  1. In Cena, click Allow Window Control. In System Settings > Privacy & Security, turn on Cena. On macOS 27 the switch is under System & App Protection Override. This lets Cena move and resize windows.
  2. Open the apps you want and put their windows where you like them.
  3. Click + at the bottom of the sidebar and give the scene a name.
  4. To go back to that scene later, select it and click Load. Cena shows first which apps it opens and which it quits.

Cena never force quits. An app with unsaved work asks you first. Finder and menu bar apps are never quit.

Cena needs macOS 26 or later, on a Mac with Apple silicon. Questions: support@spoon.com.br.
