Gist is a Mac app. Send this page to your Mac and download it there.

How to install

  1. Open the file you downloaded. Your Mac unzips it into Gist.pkg.
  2. Double-click Gist.pkg and follow the installer.
  3. Open Gist from your Applications folder.

How it works

  1. Copy the link of an article from your browser, then click Paste in Gist. You can also drag the link onto the window.
  2. Click Summarize. Gist removes menus, ads, captions and lists of other stories, and shows only the article.
  3. Move Shorten by. At 0% you read the whole text. At 90% you read only the most important 10%. Gist keeps the author's own sentences in their order and adds no words.
  4. Choose English or Portuguese in the language menu to translate. The text changes language from the top down while you read.
  5. Click Export as PDF, Share or Copy to keep the result.

Gist needs macOS 26 or later on a Mac with Apple silicon. Translation needs Apple Intelligence turned on in System Settings, Apple Intelligence & Siri. Questions: support@spoon.com.br.
