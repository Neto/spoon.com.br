Privacy Policy

Last updated November 12, 2026

Short version: our apps work on your Mac. The apps have no accounts, no analytics and no tracking. This website uses the Meta Pixel and the Reddit Pixel to measure our ads, only if you accept cookies. We get your personal data only when you buy an app or write to us.

Who we are

Spoon Software (“we”) makes the Mac apps Spin, Grabb, Gist, ReelScroll and WhatsTime and runs this website. For any privacy question, write to support@spoon.com.br.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub receives your IP address when you open a page and can keep it in its logs for security. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

When you first open this website, we ask if you accept cookies for advertising. If you decline, the Meta Pixel and the Reddit Pixel do not load. We store your choice in your browser.

If you accept, this website loads the Meta Pixel from Meta Platforms and the Reddit Pixel from Reddit. Meta and Reddit can then receive your IP address, your browser details, their cookies, the pages you open, clicks on our buy buttons and the fact that you completed a purchase. We use this to measure our ads and to show ads to people who visited this website. The legal basis is your consent. Meta and Reddit can process this data outside your country, under their own privacy policies: Meta Privacy Policy and Reddit Privacy Policy.

To change your choice at any time, click Cookies at the bottom of any page.

Spin

Spin works only on your Mac. It keeps your Spins, the files and folders in them and their positions in the app's settings on your Mac. Spin does not send your files, file names, folder names or settings to us or to anyone else.

Grabb

  • When you paste a link, Grabb connects to that website to get and download the video. That website receives your IP address, as it does when you open it in a browser.
  • Some websites only show a video when you are signed in. For these, you can choose a browser in Grabb's settings. Grabb then reads that browser's cookies on your Mac and sends them only to the website of the link. Grabb never sends cookies to us.
  • Your downloads and your download history stay on your Mac.
  • Grabb can record a diagnostic log. This is off by default. The log stays on your Mac, and we only see it if you choose to send it to us.
  • Grabb includes yt-dlp, FFmpeg and Deno. These tools run on your Mac and connect only to the website of the link you paste.

Gist

  • When you paste a link, Gist connects to that website to get the page. That website receives your IP address, as it does when you open it in a browser. This is the only connection Gist makes.
  • Gist makes the summary on your Mac. Translation uses Apple Intelligence on your Mac. The text does not go to us or to any other service.
  • Gist has no account, no license key and no analytics.

ReelScroll

  • ReelScroll controls only the browser you choose, and only its Instagram tabs. It checks when the video on screen ends and then moves to the next Reel.
  • ReelScroll does not read your messages, posts, comments or account details, and it does not save anything from Instagram.
  • ReelScroll makes no network connections of its own. It has no account, no license key and no analytics.

WhatsTime

  • WhatsTime keeps your scheduled messages and their files only on your Mac, in its own folder. We never receive them.
  • It reads your Contacts only to show names and numbers while you pick a recipient, and only if you allow it.
  • It uses Accessibility only to open the chat in WhatsApp for Mac and press Send. WhatsApp then sends the message as it does when you send it yourself.
  • WhatsTime makes no network connections of its own. It has no account, no license key and no analytics.

Updates

Once a day, Spin and Grabb check for a new version. They download a small update file from GitHub. This request includes your IP address and the name and version of the app. The apps do not send information about your Mac or about how you use them.

Purchases

We use Stripe to take payments. Stripe collects your name, email address, country and payment details, and processes them under its own privacy policy. We receive your name, email address, country and the product you bought, so we can deliver the app and help you. We never see your card number.

Email

When you write to us, we keep your message and your email address so we can answer you.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data. Our apps do not track you. We do not share your purchase records or emails for advertising. This website shares data with Meta and Reddit only if you accept cookies.

How long we keep data

We keep purchase records for as long as tax law requires. We keep emails for as long as we need them to help you, and we delete them when you ask, unless the law requires us to keep them.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the personal data we have about you, and you can object to how we use it. Write to support@spoon.com.br. We answer within 15 days. If you live in Brazil, you can also complain to the ANPD. If you live in the European Union or the United Kingdom, you can complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Our apps and this website are not made for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top.
