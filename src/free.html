How to install

  1. Open the file you downloaded. Your Mac unzips it into {{APP}}.pkg.
  2. Double-click {{APP}}.pkg and follow the installer.
  3. Open {{APP}} from your Applications folder.

How it works

  1. Copy the link of an article from your browser, then click Paste in {{APP}}. You can also drag the link onto the window.
  2. Click Summarize. {{APP}} removes menus, ads, captions and lists of other stories, and shows only the article.
  3. Move Shorten by. At 0% you read the whole text. At 90% you read only the most important 10%. {{APP}} keeps the author's own sentences in their order and adds no words.
  4. Choose English or Portuguese in the language menu to translate. The text changes language from the top down while you read.
  5. Click Export as PDF, Share or Copy to keep the result.
